About Firehouse Pools

Dedicated to Craftsmanship, Service and Community

Who We Are

More than a pool company

At Firehouse Pools, we're a firefighter-owned and operated small business built on trust, integrity and exceptional craftsmanship. Led by our owner, Taelor, who brings over 15 years of experience in the pool industry, we're passionate about creating stunning, high-quality pools that transform backyards into a personal oasis.

Our firefighter roots instill a deep sense of duty and commitment in everything we do. We understand the value of hard work, reliability and clear communication, which is why we prioritize transparency at every step of the process, from the initial consultation to the final reveal.

Our Commitment

Staying at the forefront of the industry, project after project

Taelor and our team continuously pursue advanced training and certifications to keep up with the latest techniques and innovations in pool design and construction. As a small business, we take pride in building strong relationships with our clients, treating every project as if it were our own.

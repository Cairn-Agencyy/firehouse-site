Features & Benefits

Custom outdoor kitchens, built around your lifestyle

A truly custom outdoor kitchen goes beyond the basics. We integrate premium features that enhance both the look and the experience of your outdoor living space.

  • Expanded Entertaining Space. Cook, serve and entertain outdoors without moving guests inside
  • Increased Property Value. A highly desirable feature that boosts curb appeal and resale value
  • Functional, Convenient Design. Built-in refrigeration, storage and prep space within reach
  • Energy & Cost Efficiency. Reduce indoor heat and cooling costs with energy-efficient outdoor appliances

Premium Appliances Built for Outdoor Use

Appliances that hold up to heat, moisture and daily use

Choosing the right appliances is essential to the performance and longevity of an outdoor kitchen. We install outdoor-rated grills, smokers, refrigerators and side burners designed to handle heat, moisture and frequent use, delivering consistent performance with less maintenance than indoor appliances used outdoors.

Integrated Backyard Design

Designed to flow with your patio and pool deck

Outdoor kitchens are most effective when thoughtfully integrated with surrounding outdoor spaces. We design kitchens that flow naturally with patios, pool decks and seating areas, so hosts stay connected with guests while preparing meals and traffic flow feels natural.
